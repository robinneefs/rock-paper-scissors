RPS Rivals: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

RPS Rivals is a free arcade game made by Chain Shift, a two-person hobby team. This policy covers the game on this website and the RPS Rivals app for Android. The short version: we don't collect, store or share any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, music and effects volume, CRT screen, vibration and screen scaling) are saved on your own device so the game remembers them. They never leave it, and you can remove them by clearing the app's storage or your browser's site data.

Online matches

When you play online, your device connects to our match server, which runs on Cloudflare. The server only passes the 4-letter room code and each player's moves between the two players in a room. It keeps this in memory for the length of the match and saves nothing. Like any website or online service, Cloudflare's network handles your IP address to deliver the connection; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. We don't see or keep it.

Vibration

The Android app uses two permissions: internet access, for online matches, and vibration, so the phone can buzz along with the screen shake. You can turn vibration off in Options.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the game's GitHub page.
